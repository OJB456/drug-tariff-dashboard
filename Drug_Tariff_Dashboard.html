Why DRL should analyse Drug Tariff data

  • Protect reimbursement economics: the tariff is the reimbursement reference for NHS dispensing. Tracking listed price movements and category changes helps identify where pharmacy economics and competitive net-price pressure may change.
  • Focus portfolio and tender conversations: identify molecules/formats with persistent tariff expansion, compression, or unusual volatility—useful inputs for portfolio prioritisation, launch sequencing, and payer/account discussions.
  • Detect category-risk early: a switch across tariff categories can change the pricing mechanism and comparability set. It should trigger an exception review for impacted DRL products and competitors.
  • Make the data decision-ready: tariff price is not demand, market share, acquisition cost, or DRL realised revenue. Combine this dashboard with Rx volume, market sales, supplier availability, and DRL net price before estimating value or setting commercial actions.
